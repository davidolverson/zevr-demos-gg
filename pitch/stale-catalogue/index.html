ZEVR for creators

You stopped for a while and want to start again

The hard part is not restarting. It is the first month before anything responds.

What is actually going wrong

A cold start with no pipeline usually ends the way it did last time, because the work that made it unsustainable is still there.

The first thirty days

  1. We start from the archive, so there is something to publish before you have filmed anything.
  2. The pipeline exists before the pressure does.
  3. A pace you can hold in month six, not the one you can hold in week one.

What we need from you

  • Access to what you already made.
  • Honesty about why you stopped. It usually predicts what breaks next.

What does not change

  • Everything you already built.
  • The right to stop again without owing anyone an explanation.

Straight answers

Who else is signed? Nobody. ZEVR is being built, and you would be early rather than joining something already running.

What does it cost, and what do I get paid? There are no terms on this page because ZEVR's contracting structure is not finalised. Anything we agreed would be in writing before work starts, and nobody is asking you to pay to be considered. ZEVR does not charge for entry, evaluation or trials, and never will.

What are you going to promise me? Work, not numbers. Nothing on this page says what your channel will do, because we cannot know that and neither can anyone else who tells you otherwise.
