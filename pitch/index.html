Creator pitches

Ten situations, not ten sales pages.

Pick the one that matches the creator you are about to message and send them the link. Each page names what is going wrong, what ZEVR does about it in the first thirty days, what we need from them, and what does not change. That last part is the one they are actually reading for.

How to use these

These are keyed to a situation rather than to a person, deliberately. The obvious version of this asset is ten pages each carrying a real creator's name and numbers, and that manufactures a record about somebody written by the people trying to sign them. It only has to leak once.

So personalise in the message, not on the page: one line about something public you actually watched, then the link. If you do want a named version for a specific creator, use only facts that are public and verified, and label anything forward-looking as a projection.

None of these promise an outcome, and none mention money. ZEVR cannot know what a creator's numbers will do, and its contracting structure is not finalised. A pitch that opens with either is the organisation saying something it cannot stand behind, in the one document a creator will read closely.
