ZEVR for creators

You have taken strikes, or you are one track away from one

The music in your last upload is a risk somebody should be tracking, and right now nobody is.

What is actually going wrong

Most creator pipelines keep no record of what was cleared, by whom, for what. The first time that matters is the time it costs you a channel.

The first thirty days

  1. Every piece we touch carries a rights status, and nothing publishes on UNKNOWN.
  2. Unknown means nobody checked. It is not a judgement that it is fine.
  3. You get the record itself, not a promise that we have it handled.

What we need from you

  • What you have used historically, as best you remember.
  • A willingness to drop a track when it will not clear.

What does not change

  • Your back catalogue. We are not touching it unless you ask.
  • The record, if you ever leave.

Straight answers

Who else is signed? Nobody. ZEVR is being built, and you would be early rather than joining something already running.

What does it cost, and what do I get paid? There are no terms on this page because ZEVR's contracting structure is not finalised. Anything we agreed would be in writing before work starts, and nobody is asking you to pay to be considered. ZEVR does not charge for entry, evaluation or trials, and never will.

What are you going to promise me? Work, not numbers. Nothing on this page says what your channel will do, because we cannot know that and neither can anyone else who tells you otherwise.
