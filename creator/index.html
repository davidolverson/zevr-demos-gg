Rights that are not CLEARED block publication, and UNKNOWN counts as not cleared. It is the absence of a check rather than a judgement that everything is fine, and treating absence as permission is how an agency publishes over music it never cleared. Archived and rejected work is left out of that count: it is not a problem you can clear.

Credit is attached to the person who made the work and survives their departure. Nothing on this page states or implies payment terms: ZEVR's contracting structure is not finalised, and a dashboard is not where terms get agreed.
