Two screens, built to be shown to a creator.

The first is how the team tracks everyone it is talking to. The second is what a creator gets when they sign. Both run on invented data and say so on every page, because the fastest way to lose a creator is to show them a record about themselves that somebody made up.

What these deliberately do not do

No compensation figures anywhere. ZEVR's contracting structure is not finalised, so a screen showing a creator a rate, a share or a projected payout would be a promise the organisation cannot yet stand behind. No follower counts or audience thresholds either: the public /creators page commits to there being no follower requirement, and a demo that ranks people by reach contradicts it.

No creator is signed to ZEVR today, and the one shown as Signed is invented. If a real creator asks who else is on the roster, the answer is nobody yet.
